About

# A small shop, and we like it that way.

Hi, I’m Sam Feldstein, the owner and lead developer at Presswork Web Co. I began learning web design in 2024, during a stretch where I didn’t really know what I was interested in or what I wanted to do for a living. What I did know was that I wanted to help small businesses, having worked in several of them myself.

I built my first website because I wanted a place to showcase my writing and photography, but I didn’t want the overhead of WordPress or the cost of Squarespace. The more I built, the more I liked it.

I also noticed how many small business sites were outdated, or built with site builders that pile on code nobody needs. They end up slow and bloated, with no care and no personality. I thought I could do better, so in 2026 I founded Presswork Web Co. I chose the name because websites are the modern version of the printing press, and the people who build them are a bit like modern printers. Maybe the metaphor is a stretch, but I like it.

I like working with small businesses because I like talking to people, not companies. I’ll walk you through everything so you know exactly what you’re getting, and after launch I’ll make sure it keeps working. I take pride in building sites that last.
