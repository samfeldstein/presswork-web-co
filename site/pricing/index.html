How we price.

Most of the work happens once, whatever the size of the site. Designing, building, and launching the site all go into the first page, which is why it's $800.

For additional pages, we try to scale the price to the work involved. The first few pages of a site are typically unique. A pricing page often has elements of its own, like a table or a services menu; an about page is different again, often with photos and a bio or business description; a contact page includes a contact form, and so on. That's why pages 2 to 5 are $300 each.

After that, each page is mostly adding content to a design that's already built, and the work is routine enough that each one is $150.

A page that needs far more work than a typical content page, such as one with a very large amount of content or custom interactive parts, is quoted before we start, and nothing changes without your written approval. Other services, like finding stock photos, are quoted separately, according to our base rate of $65 an hour.

FAQ.

What counts as a page?

A page is any part of your site with its own address and its own purpose, like your home page, an about page, or a page for each service you offer. Pages built automatically from a shared template, like blog posts, don't count. Legal pages, like a privacy policy, are always free.

Do I need to supply the text and photos?

You can write the text yourself, or we can write it for you for $100 a page. If we write it, we'll start with an interview about your business. Then we'll draft the text and send it to you for approval before it goes live. We don't do photography, but we can help you find stock photos at $65 an hour. Otherwise, photos come from you or a photographer you hire.

Do I need the care plan?

No, it's optional. Without it, your site stays online at no charge, and changes after launch are billed at $65 an hour. With the care plan, $99 a month covers unlimited edits, backups, security and software updates, and keeping your Google Business Profile current. Either way, questions are always free. We only charge for the actual work.

What happens if I cancel the care plan?

You can cancel at any time with 30 days' notice. Your site stays online with us at no charge, and you can still come to us for changes at $65 an hour.

Who owns my site and domain?

You do. Once the final payment is made, the design, code, text, and photos made for your site are yours. Some of the code we reuse on every site stays ours, but you can keep using it as part of your site, even if you move it to another developer. If we buy your domain for you, it's registered in your name, and its yearly renewal is billed each January at cost.

Can I move my site somewhere else later?

Yes. We'll hand over your site's files and your domain at no charge. If you'd like help setting up with a new host or developer, that's billed at $65 an hour.
