Button trials

Three hover and press animations kept from a longer list (the others were A fade, B wipe, C ink spread, D label roll and G stamp, since removed). The resting buttons are identical to the site’s. E is applied on the site; F and H are kept for reference. Hover each one, then click and hold.

E · Arrow (applied)The label slides left and an arrow slides in beside it. Tells people “this goes somewhere,” which suits the link buttons (“Book a call”, “Learn more”). On a form’s Send button it would be a little odd.

F · LiftRises 4 px with a soft glow beneath it, then drops flat and fast when pressed. Physical and calm. The glow is gold on outline buttons, cream on solid ones.

H · SheenFill fades in as usual, and a band of light sweeps across once, like a glint on a freshly printed foil. Subtle, and it adds a little shine to the gold buttons.
