Two ways to break up the black below the hero, kept from a longer list (the removed ones were A flat, C follow the light, D halftone, E sheen, G scroll-linked and H bands). F is applied on the site; B is kept for reference. The hero is a labelled strip and stays as it is. Scroll inside each window. The letters match the original eight.

Reduce Motion is on for this computer. The real site will keep these effects still for you. Here they are forced on so you can judge them. Uncheck "Show motion anyway" at the top to see the reduced-motion version.

B · Drifting glowsThree large, soft plum blobs (and one faint gold) wander behind the sections, 14–18 seconds a lap. The page always feels lit from somewhere that’s moving. Cheapest to build.

F · Gold dust (applied)Forty tiny gold specks twinkle on and off and rise a few pixels, like dust in a projector beam. Adds sparkle without changing the overall darkness.
