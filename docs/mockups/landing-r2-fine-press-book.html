You don’t have to start over.

A good book gets a second printing with the errors fixed, not a new author. Most websites are the same. I look at what you have and tell you which of three it needs.

  • ✓

    Keep it

    If it’s working, I’ll say so, and we work on getting you found instead.

  • §

    Fix what’s there

    Speed, how it reads on a phone, and how it shows up in search. That includes sites on Wix or Squarespace.

  • ¶

    Rebuild it

    Only when fixing would cost you more. You hear which, and why, before you pay for anything.

How I price

There are no packages. I estimate the hours your work will take, price them at one rate plus a margin for emails and back-and-forth, then check the total against what the work is worth to you. If it comes out higher than the job is worth, I cut the scope, not the rate.

A build is one fixed price, half at the start and half at launch. Monthly help is a written list of what I’ll do each month, priced the same way.

Chapter vii

Questions

Do you use AI?

Yes, as a tool for routine work, such as a first draft of a blog post. I edit everything, you approve anything that gets published, and every proposal says where I use it.

What does getting found in AI search mean?

More people now ask tools like ChatGPT or Google’s AI answers for a business near them. The work that helps you in Google, clear pages and accurate details on your site and Business Profile, is what those tools read too.

Can you promise I’ll rank first?

No. I don’t guarantee rankings, leads, or ad results. I promise the work, written down month by month.

Who owns the site?

You do, once the final payment is in.
