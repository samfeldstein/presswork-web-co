How it starts

Two free steps first. You pay only once we both know what the job is.

  1. Free website check

    Free, and optional

    A short email with the three things I’d fix first on your website and Business Profile, and why each matters to your customers.

  2. Free call

    Free, 20 to 30 minutes

    We talk about what you need and whether I’m the right fit. If you had a check, we go through it.

  3. Assessment and plan

    A fixed price, quoted up front

    For anything beyond a website, or a large site: I look closely at your site, your search, and your competitors, then write a plan and a proposal. The plan is yours to keep, hired or not, and the fee counts toward the work if you sign within 60 days.

For a website on its own, the call goes straight to a proposal.

Questions

Yes, as a tool. It helps me draft and check work faster. I read and approve every sentence and every change before it reaches you, and you approve anything that goes live. I never put your private data into an AI tool.

It’s part of getting found. I work on your site, Business Profile, and listings so search tools, AI ones included, can read them clearly. No one can promise a placement.
