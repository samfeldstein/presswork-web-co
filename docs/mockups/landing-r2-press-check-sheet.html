What I do, in three parts

The website

A fast site that works on every phone, and someone to call when it needs changing.

  • Design and build, or a fix-up of the site you have
  • Hosting included, on my account
  • Edits, backups, and updates each month
  • Landing pages for a campaign
  • Online stores, quoted on their own

Getting found

Showing up when people nearby search Google, Maps, or an AI assistant for what you do.

  • Search basics built into every site
  • Search Console and analytics set up
  • Google Business Profile set up or cleaned up
  • Ongoing work on Google and AI search

Ongoing marketing

The regular work that keeps you visible, done for you and sent to you for approval.

  • Business Profile posts, photos, and review replies
  • Listings on Bing, Apple, Yelp, and directories
  • A simple way to ask customers for reviews
  • Blog posts, if your site has a blog
  • Google Ads
  • A monthly report and a call each quarter

How I price

Every quote starts with the work. I estimate the hours your project takes, price them at my rate with a little room for emails and calls, then check the total against what the work is worth to you.

You get one price for the job, agreed before I start. Half at signing, half at launch, and the site is yours once it’s paid.
